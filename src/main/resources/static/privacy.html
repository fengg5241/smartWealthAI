Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

SmartRAG ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our service at shansg.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our service:

  • Account information: email address and authentication credentials you provide when registering.
  • Files you upload: documents and data you upload to SmartRAG for processing and storage.
  • Usage data: basic logs such as timestamps and feature interactions, used solely for service stability and improvement.

2. Google Drive Integration

SmartRAG offers an optional Google Drive sync feature. If you choose to connect your Google Drive account, please note:

  • We request only the drive.file permission scope, which limits our access strictly to files and folders created by SmartRAG.
  • We do not access, read, or collect any pre-existing files in your Google Drive.
  • We create a dedicated sync folder (named "SmartRAG") in your Google Drive and write only the files you have explicitly uploaded to our service.
  • You can revoke Google Drive access at any time by disconnecting the integration within SmartRAG or through your Google Account permissions page.
  • Upon disconnection, we delete all stored OAuth tokens associated with your account.

SmartRAG's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the SmartRAG service.
  • To sync your uploaded files to your connected Google Drive folder when this feature is enabled.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. OAuth tokens for Google Drive are encrypted before storage.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.
